Visual-spatial perception for AI agents

Agents want to see like we do.

scroll to look inside ↓

Scrolling flies the camera into the eye’s pupil and through the dark inside, then down onto the glowing retina, past red vessels and layers of cells. The camera turns, and we stand inside the retina: a forest of strange layered trees made of neurons, on a yellow floor of rods and cones, with red capillaries winding through and light slanting down from above.

Every AI that sees should see like we do.

move around: each layer is flat, the depth comes from motion

Inspired by Santiago Ramón y Cajal’s drawings of the retina, Disney’s multiplane camera, and the films of McBess.

The science

Built on adaptive resonance.

In Stephen Grossberg’s Adaptive Resonance Theory, seeing is a loop, not a pipeline. What the eye brings in is matched against what the brain expects. Where the two agree, the circuit resonates, and only then does it learn. So it can learn something new without forgetting what it knew, and it can tell when something doesn’t fit.

sehn is inspired by ART (Adaptive Resonance Theory) and builds on its principles and models.

Plate III, after Stephen Grossberg: adaptive resonance, in the golden wood of V4. Light rises up the trunks of some birches, what the eye brings. A first expectation comes down from above onto other trees: it does not match, and with a flash it is reset. A second comes down: where it meets the rising light, those trees ring together, glowing in time, while the tree that was lit but not expected is quieted. Only then do the threads of expectation turn solid gold: learned.

Explanation of Plate IIIadaptive resonance, after S. Grossberg

Perception as a loop: what comes in from the eye, bottom-up, is matched against what is expected, top-down, from categories already learned. A match resonates; a mismatch resets and the search goes on. Learning happens only in resonance, so new things are learned without old ones being forgotten: Grossberg’s answer to the stability–plasticity dilemma, and the reason deep networks, which adjust everything a little at a time, can forget catastrophically.

a
bottom-upthe features the eye brings, through an adaptive filter, rising
b
top-down expectationa learned category reads out what it expects to find
c
reset and searchwhen they do not match, the category is set aside and another is tried, or a new one learned
d
resonancewhere they match, the cells synchronise and amplify each other: what we consciously see
e
the matching rulean expectation alone only primes; with what comes in it fires (two against one), and what was not expected is quieted
f
learningonly in resonance do the adaptive weights change: stable, and still able to learn
